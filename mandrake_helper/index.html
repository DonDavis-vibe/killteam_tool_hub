Faction Rules

Soulstrike: Defence dice ≤ APL is a success. Defence dice > APL is a fail. 1 is always critical success. 6 is always fail.

Shadow Passage: 1/TP, one operative WITHIN SHADOW can perform Reposition by teleporting to another shadow.

Umbral Entities: Ignore Piercing against Mandrakes. Improve Save stat by 1 when WITHIN SHADOW.

Within Shadow: Within 1" of Heavy terrain, underneath Vantage terrain, or near Shadow Portal marker.

Strategy Ploys

  • Creeping Horror: Free Dash if starting and ending WITHIN SHADOW.
  • Gloaming Shroud: Retain one defence dice as normal success when WITHIN SHADOW.
  • Blade in the Dark: Can Charge while Concealed if starting or ending WITHIN SHADOW.
  • Inescapable Nightmare: Re-roll one attack dice when WITHIN SHADOW.

Firefight Ploys

  • Slither Out of Sight: At end of activation, change order to Conceal if WITHIN SHADOW.
  • Soul Feast: Heal Wounds equal to Enemy APL * successful damage dice.
  • Nowhere to Hide: Move through terrain features as if they weren't there (fly).
  • Shadow's Bite: Resolve the first attack dice as defender if WITHIN SHADOW.
