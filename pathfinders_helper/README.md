# Mandrakes Kill Team Helper

Welcome to the Mandrakes Kill Team Datapad! This is a custom-built digital companion designed specifically for playing the Mandrakes Kill Team in Warhammer 40,000: Kill Team.

## How to Use

1. **Open the App**: Simply double-click the `index.html` file to open it in any modern web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge, etc.). You don't need an internet connection to use it!
2. **Assemble Team Tab**: Build your roster. Select exactly 9 operatives (1 Nightfiend and 8 other operatives). Once 9 operatives are selected, the **Start Match** button will unlock.
3. **Match Tracker Tab**: Use the `-` and `+` buttons to easily track the wounds of your operatives during the match. Click the skull icon next to an operative's name to instantly mark them as incapacitated. Click the accordion to view their abilities.
4. **Soul Harvest / Tokens Tab**: Use this tab to track all of your markers, including Soul Harvest points, Balefire tokens, and Shadow Portals. You can add as many trackers as you need.
5. **Rules Tab**: Contains a quick reference guide for your faction rules, strategy ploys, and firefight ploys.

## Features
- Complete database of Mandrakes operatives and stats.
- Accurate Faction, Ploy, and Ability text from the rules.
- Fast, dark-mode inspired interface perfect for shadowy gameplay.
- Everything runs completely locally in your browser.

## Using on a Smartphone (Android)

Since this app runs locally without a web server, here are the easiest ways to use it on your Android phone:

### Method 1: Transfer the folder
1. Unzip the `mandrake_helper.zip` file on your computer.
2. Send the entire `mandrake_helper` folder to your Android phone (via Google Drive, USB cable, or Quick Share).
3. On your phone, open your **File Manager** (e.g., "Files by Google" or "My Files").
4. Navigate to where you saved the folder and tap on the `index.html` file.
5. Choose your preferred web browser (like Chrome) to open it.

### Method 2: Extract directly on your phone
1. Send the `mandrake_helper.zip` file directly to your phone.
2. Open your Android **File Manager** app.
3. Locate the `.zip` file, tap on it, and select **Extract**.
4. Once extracted, open the newly created folder and tap `index.html` to open it in your browser.

*(Pro Tip: When you have it open in Chrome on Android, tap the three dots in the top right menu and select **"Add to Home screen"**. This creates an app icon on your phone so you can open the helper tool quickly just like a native app!)*
